Shared art direction

Append this style anchor to each subject prompt if new versions are generated:

Warm, slightly desaturated colour grade; soft natural window light or warm tungsten ambient light; photorealistic editorial food photography; shallow depth of field; 50 mm lens; authentic and unstaged. Avoid glossy stock lighting, text, logos, watermarks, illustration, cartoon styling, oversaturation, plastic-looking AI food, and distorted hands.

Suggested negative terms where supported: text, watermark, logo, signature, illustration, cartoon, 3D render, oversaturated, neon, plastic texture, stock-photo lighting, studio softbox, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry, low quality.

The source brief suggests aspect ratios of 4:5 for mains/salads/Egyptian/Syrian dishes/desserts, 1:1 for mezze/soups/sides/social images, 3:4 for drinks, 4:3 for branches, 21:9 for most hero banners and 16:9 for the loyalty banner. The original source discusses image-generation tools and commercial terms; permissions and tool terms should be checked for the exact account and publication context before external use.
